Research

My work combines high-fidelity structural simulation, hazard engineering, and deep learning to study infrastructure performance under extreme loading.

Wind-borne Debris Impact to Transmission Infrastructure

Graduate Thesis · Oklahoma State University · Advisor: Dr. Brian D. Giffin
  • Developed a fine-scale steel angle section in LS-DYNA and performed wind-borne debris impact simulation and member-level fragility analysis.
  • Conducted nonlinear structural performance analysis of an electric transmission tower under wind loading.
  • Integrated LS-DYNA with OpenSees to perform multi-scale wind-borne debris impact analysis.
  • Developed a machine-learning-based surrogate model for LS-DYNA impact analysis.

Post-Tornado Building Damage Assessment Using Computer Vision

Graduate Thesis · Oklahoma State University · Advisor: Dr. Maha Kenawy
  • Developed a computer vision algorithm to detect and classify building damages after tornado in Barnsdall, Oklahoma.
