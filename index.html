Research

My work combines high-fidelity structural simulation, hazard engineering, and machine learning to study infrastructure performance under extreme loading.

Wind-borne Debris Impact to Transmission Infrastructure

Graduate Thesis · Oklahoma State University · Advisor: Dr. Brian D. Giffin
  • Developed a fine-scale steel angle section in LS-DYNA and performed wind-borne debris impact simulations and member-level fragility analysis.
  • Conducted nonlinear structural performance analysis of an electric transmission tower under wind loading.
  • Integrated LS-DYNA with OpenSees for multi-scale wind-borne debris impact analysis of transmission infrastructure.
  • Developed a machine-learning-based surrogate model for LS-DYNA impact analysis.

Computational Mechanics

High-fidelity nonlinear finite-element analysis and impact simulation using LS-DYNA.

Structural Engineering

Member- and system-level assessment of transmission infrastructure under extreme wind and debris loading.

Machine Learning

Surrogate models designed to accelerate computationally intensive engineering simulations.
