I am a civil engineering researcher at Oklahoma State University working at the intersection of structural engineering, natural hazards, computational mechanics, wind engineering, and machine learning.

My research focuses on understanding structural performance under extreme hazards and developing computational and data-driven methods for efficient engineering assessment.
